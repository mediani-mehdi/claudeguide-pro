# Productivity Hacks with Claude

Time is your most valuable resource. Here are proven workflows that let Claude handle the heavy lifting so you can focus on what matters.

## Morning Routine: Email Triage

Start your day by pasting your unread emails into Claude:

```
Here are my 12 unread emails. For each one:
1. Summarize in one sentence
2. Rate urgency (🔴 high / 🟡 medium / 🟢 low)  
3. Draft a response if one is needed
4. Flag any that need a meeting instead
```

**Time saved:** 30-45 minutes daily.

## Meeting Preparation

Before any meeting, give Claude the context:

```
I have a meeting in 1 hour with the product team about Q3 priorities.
Here are last quarter's results: [paste data]
Here's the current roadmap: [paste outline]

Help me prepare:
1. Three key talking points
2. Two questions to ask the team
3. A proposed priority framework
4. Potential objections and my responses
```

## Research Synthesis

Instead of reading 10 articles, have Claude do it:

```
I've gathered these 5 articles about remote work trends.
[paste or summarize each]

Please:
1. Identify the common themes across all articles
2. Note any contradictions between sources
3. Summarize the key findings in 3 paragraphs
4. Suggest 3 actionable takeaways for my team
```

## Weekly Review Template

```
Here's what I accomplished this week:
[paste your task list / notes]

Help me create:
1. A concise summary for my manager (3-4 sentences)
2. My top 3 priorities for next week
3. Any blockers I should escalate
4. A "wins" list for my personal records
```

## Content Creation Pipeline

For any content you create regularly:

```
I need to write a LinkedIn post about [topic].
My audience is [describe audience].
My voice is professional but conversational.

Give me:
1. A hook (first line that stops the scroll)
2. Three key points with brief explanations
3. A call to action
4. 5 relevant hashtags
```

## The 80/20 Rule with Claude

Focus Claude on tasks where it provides the most leverage:

| High Leverage | Low Leverage |
|---|---|
| Drafting first versions | Final proofreading |
| Research synthesis | Checking single facts |
| Template creation | One-off formatting |
| Data analysis | Simple calculations |
| Brainstorming | Tasks you enjoy doing |

## Quick Wins You Can Start Today

1. **Email templates** - Create 10 templates for your most common emails
2. **Meeting notes** - Paste raw notes, get structured summaries
3. **Weekly reports** - Automate your status updates
4. **SOPs** - Document your processes for your team
5. **Learning** - Get personalized explanations of complex topics

---

*The goal isn't to automate everything - it's to automate the tedious so you can focus on the meaningful.*
