# Getting Started with Claude AI

Welcome to the world of Claude AI. Whether you're a complete beginner or transitioning from another AI assistant, this guide will get you up and running in minutes.

## Creating Your Account

Getting started with Claude is straightforward:

1. Visit [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)
2. Sign up with your email or Google account
3. Verify your email address
4. You're ready to start

### Free vs. Pro

Claude offers both free and paid tiers. The free tier is generous enough for most personal use, but the Pro plan unlocks longer conversations, priority access, and early access to new features.

## Your First Conversation

The most common mistake beginners make is treating Claude like a search engine. Instead of asking "What is machine learning?", try something more engaging:

```
I'm a graphic designer who keeps hearing about machine learning. 
Can you explain it in terms I'd understand, maybe using design 
analogies? I want to know if it's relevant to my work.
```

This approach gives Claude:
- **Context** about who you are
- **Framing** for how to explain
- **Purpose** for why you're asking

## Essential Tips for Beginners

### Be Specific
Vague prompts get vague answers. Instead of "write me an email," try "write a professional email to my team announcing a project delay, keeping the tone reassuring and action-oriented."

### Provide Examples
If you want output in a specific style, show Claude what you mean:

```
Write a product description in this style:
"The Horizon Watch isn't just a timepiece - it's a statement. 
Crafted from brushed titanium, it whispers sophistication."

Now write one for a leather notebook.
```

### Iterate and Refine
Your first prompt doesn't have to be perfect. Start broad, then narrow:

1. "Help me plan a birthday party"
2. "Make it a surprise party for a 30-year-old who loves hiking"
3. "Budget is $500, 15 guests, outdoor venue preferred"

Each refinement gets you closer to exactly what you need.

### Use Conversation History
Claude remembers your entire conversation. Build on previous responses:

- "Can you expand on point #3?"
- "That's good, but make it more casual"
- "Now combine the best parts of both versions"

| Brainstorming | Ideas, strategies, creative concepts |

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Don't ask yes/no questions** when you want detailed answers
2. **Don't assume Claude knows your context** - always provide background
3. **Don't accept the first response** if it's not quite right - iterate
4. **Don't forget to specify format** - bullet points, paragraphs, tables, etc.
