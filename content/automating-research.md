# Automating Your Research

Research doesn't have to be a time sink. With the right Claude workflows, you can synthesize information faster than ever while maintaining quality and depth.

## The Research Framework

### 1. Define Your Question
Before asking Claude anything, get crystal clear on what you're researching:
- What specifically do I need to know?
- What will I do with this information?
- How deep do I need to go?

### 2. Gather Sources
Collect your sources - articles, papers, reports, data - and organize them by relevance.

### 3. Systematic Analysis
Feed each source to Claude with consistent instructions:

```
Analyze this article and extract:
1. Main thesis (1 sentence)
2. Key supporting evidence (3-5 points)
3. Methodology used
4. Limitations noted by the author
5. Relevance to my research question: [your question]
```

### 4. Synthesis
Once you have individual analyses, ask Claude to connect the dots:

```
Here are analyses of 6 articles on [topic].
[paste analyses]

Create a research synthesis that:
- Identifies common themes across sources
- Highlights contradictions or debates
- Notes gaps in the current research
- Provides actionable conclusions
```

## Research Templates by Type

### Market Research
```
Analyze this market data and provide:
- Market size and growth trajectory
- Key players and market share
- Emerging trends (next 2-3 years)
- Opportunities for a [your company type]
- Risks and potential disruptions
```

### Competitive Analysis
```
Compare [Company A] and [Company B]:
- Product offerings and pricing
- Target audience overlap
- Unique value propositions
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Market positioning
Present as a detailed comparison table.
```

### Literature Review
```
I've collected 10 papers on [topic].
For each paper, I'll share the abstract and key findings.
After reviewing all papers, create a literature review that:
- Groups papers by methodology/approach
- Identifies the evolution of thinking on this topic
- Highlights the current consensus and open questions
```

## Tips for Better Research

1. **Always verify** - Claude can synthesize, but check critical facts
2. **Be specific** about the depth and format you need
3. **Use consistent templates** for comparable analysis across sources
4. **Ask for citations** - "which source supports this claim?"
5. **Iterate** - first pass for breadth, second for depth
