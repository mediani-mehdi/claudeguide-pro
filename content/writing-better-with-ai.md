# Writing Better with AI

Claude isn't here to replace your voice - it's here to amplify it. Here's how to use AI as a writing partner that makes your best work even better.

## The AI Writing Framework

### 1. Ideation
Start by brainstorming with Claude. Share your topic and audience, then ask for angles you might not have considered.

### 2. Outline
Have Claude create a structured outline. Be specific about depth and format.

### 3. Draft
Write the first draft yourself, or let Claude take the first pass while you provide direction. Either way, the next step is where the magic happens.

### 4. Revision
This is where Claude truly shines. Feed it your draft with specific instructions:

```
Review this draft for:
- Clarity: Are any sentences confusing?
- Flow: Does each paragraph connect to the next?
- Voice: Does it sound natural, not robotic?
- Impact: Is the opening strong? Is the ending memorable?
```

### 5. Polish
Final pass for grammar, word choice, and rhythm. Ask Claude to tighten sentences without losing meaning.

## Finding Your Voice with AI

The key is teaching Claude your style:

```
Here are three paragraphs I've written that represent my best work:
[paste examples]

Analyze my writing style: tone, sentence length, vocabulary level,
use of metaphors, and any distinctive patterns.

Now write a paragraph about [topic] in my exact style.
```

## Types of Writing Claude Excels At

- **Blog posts** - especially outlines and first drafts
- **Documentation** - technical writing that's clear and complete
- **Emails** - professional communications with the right tone
- **Social media** - platform-specific content with hooks
- **Reports** - data-driven narratives with structure
